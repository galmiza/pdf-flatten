# Privacy policy

*PDF Flattener: last updated November 17, 2026*

PDF Flattener collects no data.

- **No internet access.** The app does not request the Internet permission: it cannot send anything anywhere.
- **Documents stay on your device.** A PDF is read only when you choose it in the file picker or share it to the app. It is processed on your device, and only the latest document and its flattened copy are kept, in the app's private storage, until you open the next one. The flattened file is written only where you choose to save it, or handed to the app you choose to share it with.
- **Only your settings are stored**, on your device: which elements to merge and the file name suffix.
- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDK** that collects data.
