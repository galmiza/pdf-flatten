PDF Flattener makes signatures, handwriting, text boxes and form fields a permanent part of your PDF pages, so whoever opens the document sees them.

WHY?
When you sign or annotate a PDF on your phone, most apps add a floating layer on top of the page instead of drawing on it. Many viewers, mail previews and browsers don't display these layers: your signature simply disappears for the recipient, and filled form fields can show up empty.

WHAT IT DOES
PDF Flattener merges these layers into the page itself:
• Handwritten signatures, ink and stamps
• Typed text boxes
• Highlights, underlines and shapes
• Form fields (or keep the form fillable, your choice)

The result stays sharp: the drawing stays vector, text remains selectable and the file keeps about the same size. Nothing is turned into a blurry image.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose a PDF, or share it to PDF Flattener from any app.
2. Check the result: switch between the original and the flattened version.
3. Save the copy (name_flattened.pdf) or share it right away.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline: the app has no internet access
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your documents never leave your phone

ALSO
• Password-protected PDFs
• Choose what to merge and the file name suffix
• Light and dark themes
• English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch
